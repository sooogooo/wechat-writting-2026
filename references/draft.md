# Draft Mode

## Objective

Write a complete WeChat article that integrates a clear judgment, concrete scene, mechanism, consequence, and executable decision framework.

The article must be accessible to ordinary readers and credible to professionals. It must not become generic science popularization, project promotion, a textbook, or an industry report.

## Default deliverable

1. Three alternative titles (use the `title` reference's slot-A style — one each: 传播 / 判断 / 克制).
2. Cover copy under 100 Chinese characters.
3. Complete article (default 1,800–2,200 Chinese characters when length is unspecified).

## Opening

Enter through one of these — never with broad industry background:

- a real scene
- a financial / operating number
- a consumer question
- a clinical detail
- a counterintuitive judgment
- an industry contradiction
- a failed case
- an uncomfortable fact

State the central judgment within the first 3–5 paragraphs.

## Development (use flexibly, do not label the steps)

1. Phenomenon — show a familiar, concrete situation.
2. Misunderstanding — name the common wrong explanation.
3. Mechanism — explain the medical / aesthetic / psychological / operational / structural cause.
4. Cost — show what the wrong judgment produces.
5. New judgment — give a better framework.
6. Action — give specific, sequenced, testable recommendations.

## Subheadings

Short, judgment-led. Examples:

- 最难卖的,不一定是效果最差的
- 项目出了厂,生意还没有开始
- 机构拒绝的不是产品,是后续成本
- 脸没有变老,结构先失去了秩序
- 真正昂贵的,是每一次都从零开始

Forbidden: "问题分析" / "原因一" / "解决方案" / "总结" / "第一部分" / "综上所述".

## Recommendations

Do not stop at "提升服务 / 强化专业 / 加强管理". Explain:

- what to do
- why it matters
- what to do first
- how to know whether it works (success criteria)
- what should NOT be done

## Ending

End with a concrete judgment, scene, unresolved question, cost, or restrained line. No summary slogans, no blessings, no forced elevation, no repeat of the whole argument.

## Formatting (default markdown → WeChat)

Use H2 (`##`) for subheadings. Avoid H3 unless the section is genuinely long. Use single newlines between paragraphs. Do not insert drafting notes inside the article. Do not explain compliance with these instructions inside the article.

## Output template

```
### 三个备选标题
1. ……
2. ……
3. ……

### 封面文案
（≤ 100 字）

## 正文标题

[完整正文]
```
